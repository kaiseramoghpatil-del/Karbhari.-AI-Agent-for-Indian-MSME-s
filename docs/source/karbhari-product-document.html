At a glance

An investigator, not a chatbot.

Most Indian MSMEs run on a bank cash-credit facility whose usable limit, Drawing Power, is recalculated from stock, debtors and creditors every month. When those numbers are stale, mis-aged, duplicated or simply mis-read, the business quietly loses access to capital it is entitled to under its own sanction terms. Nobody inside a small enterprise has the time or specialist skill to check. KARBHARI does.

01 · Why KARBHARI exists

Large companies have departments.
MSMEs have the owner.

Big enterprises do not win only because of software. They win because they employ specialists in every domain (treasury, credit, collections, compliance, analytics) who understand problems that cut across all of them. The owner of a small Indian enterprise has to be all of those people at once.

The specialist-capability gap

An MSME may have accounting software, a bank portal and a part-time accountant. What it lacks is someone who can look across all of them, understand what a problem actually involves, and act on it. The real challenge is not "MSMEs need better software". It is that small businesses lack the specialist capacity to understand and coordinate complex, cross-functional problems.

KARBHARI's long-term vision is to be that missing operator: a problem-first layer where the owner brings a real situation in their own words, and does not need to know which department, professional or software category it belongs to.

Why working capital first

Working capital is where this gap hurts most and is easiest to prove. A cash-credit facility's usable limit moves every month with the stock statement and the debtor book. If the documents behind it are inconsistent, the owner sees only a smaller number at the bank, with no way of knowing whether it is right.

Where the number goes wrong
Five ways Drawing Power is mis-stated
  1. Stale or swinging stock statements. A large month-on-month swing, or a statement older than the facility allows, makes the stock figure unreliable.
  2. Debtors aged incorrectly. Invoices past the sanctioned cut-off (e.g. 90 days) must be excluded; valid ones must not be.
  3. Duplicate ledger entries. The same invoice entered twice inflates the debtor book.
  4. Receipts that don't match invoices. Untagged payments, partial payments and over-payments distort what is truly outstanding.
  5. Assumed rather than sanctioned terms. Margins and creditor deductions that differ from the sanction letter change the answer.
Errors run in both directions. KARBHARI is not built to argue for a bigger number; it is built to find the right one, and to say clearly when the evidence does not support a claim.
Under RBI's IRACP norms, Drawing Power based on a stock statement more than three months old is irregular; if the irregularity continues for 90 days the account must be classified NPA. KARBHARI states this rule whenever staleness is relevant.

KARBHARI   Working Capital Guardian03 · Drawing Power
03 · Drawing Power, explained

The number your bank uses,
and how KARBHARI rebuilds it.

A cash-credit facility is sanctioned up to a limit, but the business can only draw what its current collateral supports. That figure is Drawing Power. KARBHARI computes it in exactly one place in the codebase, calculations/drawing_power.py.

Stock and book-debt hypothecation · standard formula
DP = Stock × (1 − stock margin) + Eligible debtors × (1 − debtor margin) − Creditors
…then capped between ₹0 and the sanctioned limit. Gaps under ₹1,000 are treated as rounding noise, not findings.

Worked example: the verified Suryoday Textiles case

Every gap in the inputs is said out loud

If an input is missing, the engine does not quietly guess. Each fallback is written into assumptions_used and carried into the finding:

  • No sanctioned margins found → typical 25% / 40% used, and flagged as a placeholder to confirm.
  • No ageing breakdown → total debtors used, and flagged as an upper bound.
  • No creditor figure → treated as zero, and flagged as optimistic.
  • Collateral above the limit → capped, and the cap is recorded.

Any assumption downgrades the verdict

A positive gap is graded supported only when no assumptions were needed. Otherwise it is unresolved. If the calculated figure is at or below what the bank recognises, the finding is contradicted: the evidence does not support a claim of lost capacity.

The gap is framed as headroom within the existing sanctioned limit: never a request for more credit, and never a guarantee the bank will release it.

KARBHARI   Working Capital Guardian04 · How it works
04 · How it works

The model reasons.
Python calculates.

KARBHARI separates judgement from arithmetic. A language model does what it is good at: reading messy documents, deciding what to look at next, and structuring line items. Every number that matters is produced by deterministic code that cannot hallucinate.

Enforced in code · 1

Headline built by the system

The Drawing Power gap finding is constructed by guardian.py straight from the engine's return value. The agent is told not to restate it and has no way to alter it.

Enforced in code · 2

Reconciled debtors win

If debtors were reconciled, calculate_drawing_power overrides whatever eligible-debtor figure the agent passes in, and records the override where it can be seen.

Enforced in code · 3

Per-debtor isolation

Reconciliation groups records by debtor before matching, so one customer's payment can never settle another customer's invoice, whatever the agent sends.

KARBHARI   Working Capital Guardian05 · The loop
05 · The investigation loop

A real investigation,
not a fixed pipeline.

Each turn, the model sees the running transcript and returns exactly one action: call a tool, or finish. It decides which documents to read, in what order, and when it has enough. A required checklist keeps it thorough; the step limit keeps it bounded.

1
list_evidence

Inventory the case

See every document attached: ID, filename and category.

2
read_evidence × every item

Read everything, no exceptions

The agent must read every item, even ones that look redundant. A document it did not read cannot inform its conclusion. Figures it uses must be traceable to a quote it actually saw.

3
check_consistency

Compare periods

If two documents or periods report the same field (two stock statements a month apart is the classic case), flag any swing of 20% or more for a human to explain.

4
reconcile_debtors

Rebuild the debtor book

When invoice- or receipt-level data exists, never trust a ledger's subtotal. Reconstruct eligible debtors from line items, as of the evidence's own date.

5
calculate_drawing_power

Compute, or explain why not

Run the engine with the facility terms and figures found. If inputs are missing, it says exactly which ones.

6
action: finish

Report findings

Everything except the headline gap, which the system adds itself: data-quality issues, ambiguities, and anything that cuts against a favourable conclusion.

ONE STEP, AS THE MODEL RETURNS IT
{
  "thought": "Two stock statements cover
    Aug and Sep. Compare them
    before trusting Sep alone.",
  "action": "call_tool",
  "tool": "check_consistency",
  "tool_input": {
    "field_history": {
      "stock_value": [
        {"period_label": "Aug-26", "value": 4200000},
        {"period_label": "Sep-26", "value": 5800000}
      ]
    }
  },
  "findings": null
}
→ 1 variance flag: stock value +38.1%
Loop mechanics
  • Bounded: 20 tool calls maximum, so cost and runtime stay predictable.
  • Resilient: one shared retry budget absorbs API errors and malformed or corrupted JSON, both observed in real runs.
  • Forgiving where safe: if the model mistakenly "calls" finish_investigation as a tool, it is treated as a finish, not a wasted step.
  • Recorded: every step's thought, tool, input and output is saved as the trace.
  • Honest stop reasons: complete, incomplete (step limit or invalid action), or error.

06 · Features

What it does, and why.

01

Case and evidence workspace

Create a case per business, drop in evidence and tag each file with a category. A completeness checklist shows what is still missing.

  • Reads PDF, XLSX, CSV and plain text
  • Each document bounded to 20,000 characters for predictable prompts
  • Scanned PDFs without a text layer are flagged plainly and never crash the run
WHYReal MSME records arrive in every format. A bad file becomes a visible gap, not a broken investigation.
02

Bounded agentic investigator

A ReAct-style loop in which the model chooses which documents to read, which checks to run, and when to stop, up to 20 steps.

  • Required checklist: read everything, compare periods, rebuild debtors, compute
  • Single JSON action protocol with retries
  • Provider-agnostic LLM client (Gemini today)
WHYEvery case is shaped differently. A fixed pipeline breaks on the second case; an unbounded agent is expensive and unpredictable.
03

Deterministic debtor reconciliation

Rebuilds outstanding and eligible debtors from invoices and receipts, per debtor, as of the evidence's own date.

  • Three-tier matching: invoice-tagged → FIFO against the same debtor's oldest open invoices → explicitly unallocated
  • Duplicate invoices detected and excluded, with the amount shown
  • Ageing checked against the sanctioned cut-off (e.g. 90 days)
WHYA ledger subtotal is someone else's arithmetic. Tally-style ledgers rarely tag receipts, so FIFO by debtor is how a credit analyst would do it.
04

Period-over-period consistency

Flags any field (stock, debtors, creditors) that moves 20% or more between consecutive periods, and any figure that appears from zero.

  • Threshold configurable per call
  • Flags, never explains: the agent and the human judge the cause
WHYA big swing may be growth, seasonality or a data error. It should never pass silently into a Drawing Power figure.
05

Drawing Power engine

The single place the number is computed: sanctioned margins, eligible debtors, creditor deduction, capped at the limit, compared with the bank's figure.

  • Every fallback recorded in assumptions_used
  • Missing inputs listed by name and source document
  • ₹1,000 materiality threshold
WHYMoney arithmetic must be exact, repeatable and testable. Language models are none of those things.
06

Graded findings with citations

Each finding carries a status, a plain-language explanation, the rupee impact where known, evidence IDs and exact quotes.

supported unresolved ineligible / contradicted
WHYAn owner taking a finding to the bank needs to know how sure it is, and exactly which line of which document backs it.

07

"How it investigated": the full trace

Every step the agent took is shown as a timeline: its reasoning, the tool it chose, the exact input, the raw output and a one-line summary.

  • Generated live from the actual run, not reconstructed afterwards
  • Stored with the investigation for later review
WHY"Trust me" is not good enough for money. An owner, accountant or banker should be able to follow the reasoning step by step.
08

Honesty guardrails

The investigator is told to look actively for what undermines a favourable result (stale statements, duplicates, unallocated receipts, unexplained swings) and to report it.

  • No numeric claim a tool didn't produce
  • No claims about what a bank will do
  • "Appears supportable under the supplied facility terms", never "the bank owes you"
WHYAn advocacy engine would be useless in front of a credit officer. Credibility is the product.
09

Coverage backstop

If the case has several period-specific documents of the same category but the agent never ran a consistency check, the system adds an explicit unresolved finding saying so.

  • Never invents a variance; only reports the gap in coverage
  • Based on a cheap deterministic signal, not a re-read
WHYWhere the agent's discipline was observed to be unreliable, the gap is made visible in code instead of hoping a prompt fixes it.
10

Film-grade investigation console

A dark financial-investigation console with a persistent rail: Findings · Investigation · Evidence · How it investigated · Actions.

  • Count-up headline, KPI strip, capacity bars, eligibility ring
  • Full-screen view of the agent reading each document while it runs
  • Subtle synthesized sound effects, with a mute toggle
WHYThe answer is one number; the confidence comes from being able to drill into everything behind it, and enjoying doing so.
11

aiKart "Try Me Now" sandbox

A one-shot adapter that calls the same service layer as the web app. By default it runs a bundled six-document demo case through the real engines and returns a full markdown report.

  • Manifest: 1 CPU, 1 GB RAM, 120 s timeout
  • Network egress limited to the Gemini API
WHYOne implementation, two front doors, and a buyer sees a real result even without uploading files.
12

Packaged and tested

A single Docker image serves the API and the built console. 59 automated tests cover extraction, reconciliation, consistency, Drawing Power, tools, the agent loop, the sandbox, the API and frontend serving, plus a live model run.

  • The full 7-document case verified inside the container
  • Real bugs found in verification became permanent regression tests
WHYFor a financial tool, "it worked on my laptop once" is not evidence. Reproducibility is.

07 · Trust and guardrails

Built to be right,
not agreeable.

A tool that tells an owner what they want to hear is worse than no tool. KARBHARI's guardrails live in three places: the prompt, the tools, and the code that assembles the final result.

How a finding is graded

StatusMeaningTypical example
supportedEvidence and a tool output directly back it, with no assumptions needed.Calculated DP exceeds reported DP using sanctioned margins and reconciled debtors.
unresolvedEvidence is incomplete or ambiguous, an assumption was needed, or more documents are required.A ₹70,000 receipt that cannot be allocated to any open invoice.
ineligible / contradictedThe evidence contradicts a favourable claim, or a sanction-term condition is not met.An invoice aged 105 days against a 90-day eligibility cut-off.

Rules the investigator follows

  • Never state a number a tool didn't produce; never invent or round a figure it didn't see.
  • Actively look for what undermines a favourable conclusion, and report it.
  • Never assert what a bank will do or that a figure is legally guaranteed.
  • State the RBI IRACP staleness rule when a stock statement is more than three months old, as the general rule, not as something that has already happened.
  • If nothing was found beyond the headline, finish with no extra findings. No filler.

Rules enforced in code

  • The headline gap finding is built from the engine output, never from the agent's words.
  • Reconciled eligible debtors override the agent-supplied value, and the override is shown.
  • Records are segregated per debtor before any matching.
  • Duplicate invoices and unallocated receipts are always reported, never silently dropped.
  • Any assumption downgrades a gap from supported to unresolved.
  • A skipped consistency check becomes a visible unresolved finding.
5
real issues were found by verification and documented openly: four fixed, one mitigated. They include payments from one debtor settling another's invoice, which is now prevented in code and covered by a regression test. KARBHARI's README lists what is real, what is stubbed, and what is not yet reliable.

11 · Limitations and roadmap

What's real today,
and what comes next.

KARBHARI states its limits as plainly as its findings. These are the known gaps at v0.7.0.

Known limitations

  • The agent can still skip a check. It may skip check_consistency; the code backstop makes that gap visible, but does not force the check. A premature finish before the Drawing Power calculation is sent back once.
  • Creditor-side reconciliation is simple: one total, not invoice- and payment-level.
  • Cross-document conflicts (two documents disagreeing on one figure) are not explicitly re-detected in the agent version.
  • One case family verified in depth; not yet tested on 10+ documents, contradictory evidence, non-English text or illegible uploads.
  • No OCR for scanned PDFs; no authentication; evidence categories are advisory.
  • Run time varies with the model's response time, typically 30 seconds to 2 minutes.

Roadmap

→

Close the consistency gap

Native function-calling with enforced tool sequencing, or a stronger model tier.

→

Turn findings into action

The Actions tab, already previewed in the console: a draft, evidence-backed note to the bank requesting a Drawing Power recomputation.

→

Harder cases

Contradictory documents, more debtors, illegible uploads: find the next failure before a user does.

→

Deeper evidence

OCR for scanned statements; invoice-level creditor reconciliation; cross-document conflict detection.

→

Beyond working capital

Delayed receivables, compliance notices, supplier disputes: the same investigate-then-prove pattern, problem-first.

Deliberately out of scope for v0.7.0

TReDS and banking API integrations, autonomous actions on the owner's behalf, and multi-agent orchestration. KARBHARI investigates and recommends; the owner decides and acts.
